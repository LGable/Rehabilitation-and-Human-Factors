# Environmental Design and Patient Safety | Human Factors

**Type:** Generalized design method from clinical environment work, without patient details.

## Stage gates

1. Observe task demands and interaction with space; collect only authorized aggregate data.
2. Identify foreseeable hazards: visibility, trip risk, head-impact exposure, tipping, entrapment, sanitation, access and emergency egress.
3. Evaluate alternatives with stakeholders; set constraints and safe-use expectations.
4. Obtain organizational procurement, safety and clinical signoffs.
5. Orient staff and assign routine inspection and replacement responsibility.
6. Monitor usability and balancing measures, not just installation completion.

## Evidence boundaries

Project planning or procurement does not establish reduced incidents. Neither psychiatric sensory rooms nor a school-based regulation environment should be offered as a clinical intervention protocol without local approvals and individualized care considerations.
