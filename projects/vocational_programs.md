# Functional Vocational Programming | Program Design

**Type:** Generalized implementation model inspired by historically reported clinical café, gardening, cooking and vocational exploration programs.

## Work design

Break complex tasks into observable steps: preparation → setup → role assignment → hygiene check → production → cleanup → reflection. Track independence, amount of assistance, completion time, safety issues and participant choice without identifying individuals.

## Measurement

Count completed opportunities and training trials; explicitly define 'independent' and 'prompted'. Separate practice opportunity from skill acquisition; do not infer clinical effectiveness from products produced or program continuation.

## Operational risk

Safeguards include appropriate supervision, equipment suitability, food safety, task/role limitations, accessibility, and clear clinical ownership. Public portfolio examples are not treatment prescriptions.
