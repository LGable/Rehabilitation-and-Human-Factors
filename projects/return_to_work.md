# Government-Funded Return-to-Work Program Coordination

**Type:** Owner-reported New Zealand career experience + synthetic reporting dataset.

## Process

Referral → functional/work demands assessment → accommodation and graded plan → employer/insurer review → periodic progress → case closure. This model integrates employment participation, job redesign, work-hour progression, physical demands and interdisciplinary coordination.

Historical résumé claims approximately 89% successful return-to-work outcomes, 94% case-throughput performance, 49% reporting efficiency improvement and a 37% personal documentation-time reduction. These require source verification and precise denominators.

## Synthetic example

`return_to_work_synthetic.csv` includes fictional cases with industry, service pathway, referral-to-plan days and illustrative closure status. It is not drawn from ACC or APM records and not designed to reproduce historical outcome percentages.

## Human factors angle

Assess mismatch between job task demands, worker current capacity, fatigue, environmental barriers and feasible accommodations. Distinguish continued work with modifications from return after absence. Report practical trade-offs to employers and funders in plain language.
