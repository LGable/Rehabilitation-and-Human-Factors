# Care Coordination and Quality | Case Management

**Type:** Generalized example based on prior home-health and family-based service work.

## Workstream model

Referral → consent and intake → assessment → service plan → interdisciplinary handoff → visit/authorization tracking → review → transition or discharge.

## Example KPIs

Timeliness of intake, overdue reassessments, documentation completion, supplier turnaround, follow-up confirmation, and reconciliation of scheduled versus delivered visits.

This project uses **no real patient or family cases**. Actual historical patient counts and service rates in a résumé should be validated separately before publication.
